Phase 1: Description of the system we are redesigning, the representative tasks in order of what we will get our participants to view and interact with using the system, pre-study and post-study questions and a script of what we will ask each participant.

Phase 2: Once the interviews wih participants have been done we record description of users, followed by an artifact model, flow model, and social model highlighting trending or analogous comments made by our participants. This is also followed by a do's and don'ts of the features and attributes of the system.

Phase 3: Each of our team members created a personal redesign by making a sketch of what the redesign should look like along with a description of the decisions we made and why, followed by a final design of the sketches and assumptions for the hardware and users.

Phase 4: Our group usability evaluation report.
